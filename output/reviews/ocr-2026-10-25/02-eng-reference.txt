Tuberculosis, or TB disease, is highly contagious and can be deadly. TB germs can live in your 
body for years without causing symptoms. This is called inactive TB or latent TB infection. Without 
treatment, inactive TB can become active TB disease at any time and make you sick. Once TB 
becomes active, it can spread from person to person through the air. Getting tested and treated 
for inactive TB can protect yourself, your family and friends, and your community.
